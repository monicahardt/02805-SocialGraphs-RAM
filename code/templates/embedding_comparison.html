How the map was computed

Each point pools a character’s dialogue from installments where they speak in both media. The two final films are grouped as one installment. The roster requires at least 100 words on each side.

Each method fits its own PCA to unit-normalized book and film profiles together. The 2D views show the first two components. The 3D views for TF-IDF and BERT add the third.
