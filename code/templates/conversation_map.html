      How exchanges and words were selected

      An arrow points from the middle speaker in an A–B–A sequence to the person who speaks on both sides of that turn. Consecutive lines by one speaker are merged. The sequence breaks at changes of source or segment and at unknown or generic speakers. Group conversations can make the intended addressee ambiguous.

      The map shows the 15 characters with the most participation in inferred exchanges across both media, ranked by the smaller of their book and film totals. Arrows show pairs among those characters. Question-bearing turns contain a question mark. The final two films share installment seven.

      Distinctive words are repeated terms weighted by TF-IDF across directed pairs in the selected medium, installment, and dialogue mode. Common words and the two selected characters’ name components are filtered. Counts beside terms are raw occurrences. Excerpts favor turns containing those words and show at most one excerpt per source segment.
