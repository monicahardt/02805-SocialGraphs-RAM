How the word categories and positions work

Words use the published Hu–Liu opinion lexicon. The overlapping entry “envious” is excluded. Exact lowercase token matches count; no word categories are learned from Harry Potter characters.

Each coordinate is 1,000 × matched positive or negative word tokens ÷ all dialogue word tokens in that profile. A character’s profiles cover the same story installments; the two final films form story seven. Each profile has at least 100 words. Small markers identify profiles with fewer than 300 words.

These are vocabulary densities, not judgments of goodness or evil. The word list was developed for opinions and can misread fantasy terms, negation, sarcasm and words such as “well.” The dialogue speaker assignments are also provisional. Read the contributing lines before interpreting a character’s position.

The two word lists have different sizes, so equal positive and negative counts are not a calibrated neutral point. Unmatched words receive no polarity label. Story roles are independent display annotations.
