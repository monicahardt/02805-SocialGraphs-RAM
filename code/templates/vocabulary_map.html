What a connection means

Nodes are character vocabularies. A line means that one profile ranks the other among its nearest TF-IDF/cosine neighbors. It does not indicate a conversation between the characters. Similarity scores use the full vocabulary vectors, not distance in the drawing.

Selection shows up to three strongest actual connections to other characters. The book/film counterpart is shown separately with a dotted line. “List all” reveals incoming connections from other profiles too. Shared words are the largest contributions to the cosine score.

Each character's profiles cover the same story installments. Story labels and source speaker assignments remain provisional; sparse profiles and names can affect the results. The layout, graph and numerical analysis are unchanged by these display controls.
