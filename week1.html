          07 / Recurring link patterns

          The network fingerprint

          Download analysis ↓

          Choose a pattern

            Each example shows all links among exactly three characters; links to the rest of the network are omitted. Examples are the first up to eight triples in node-ID order, not a representative sample.

            Null model and limits

            The comparison preserves each character’s in-degree and out-degree, including isolates, but not communities, reciprocity, or connectivity. Every unordered triple is counted once, including disconnected triples in the full census. If the random sample has zero standard deviation, z is undefined and shown as N/A.
