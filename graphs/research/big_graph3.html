Neighbor retention vs. book–film TF-IDF cosine

Network neighborhoods are compared only among characters present in both media within each installment. Book–film TF-IDF uses each character’s dialogue from installments where they have text in both media, with character names and common words excluded. These are matched character-level comparisons, not matched-scene comparisons; token thresholds and aliases limit coverage.
